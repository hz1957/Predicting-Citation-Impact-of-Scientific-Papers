Predicting Citation Impact of Scientific Papers

A Regression Approach with Supervised and Unsupervised Learning

CS 7641 Machine Learning · Summer 2026 · Group Project

Progress Documents

Citation counts are a widely used — if imperfect — proxy for scientific influence, and they are extremely skewed: a small fraction of papers collect most of the citations. We build models that forecast the citation impact of AI/ML papers at (or shortly after) publication, and use feature-importance analysis to learn which signals actually drive predicted impact.
